I have bought a Steamdeck but i am still waiting for my Aliexpress dock. As i don´t have any USB-C Keyboard or an adapter i was in the struggle of can´t typing anything in desktop mode. But with this simple trick you get a virtual keyboard without typing anything, just by using the touchscreen.

- Start your Steamdeck
- Enable Developer Options
- Go to Desktop Mode by long holding the power button and selecting Desktop mode
- Open \\"Discover\\" (the App Store). It is the second App in the Taskbar
- Select Accessibility
- And press on the \\"Install\\" button of \\"CoreKeyboard\\"
- After install, press on \\"CoreKeyboard\\" to open its page and select \\"Launch\\"

Tadaaa...
